html-explain now has far more room to teach. Instead of a fixed recipe, its "Write the page" step hands the agent a toolbox and a way to choose: watch it run (a scene with Play), build it up, picture then the real thing (ELI5 layers, now one option among several), side by side, follow the numbers, or a tour of the map. It pushes the agent to draw the mechanism, with at least one moving or playable drawing when the topic has moving parts, and says two explain pages should not look alike.

- **"Questions you might have"** replaces the "What's still unclear?" box: 4–8 questions a sharp reader would ask, each a fold that opens to a short answer and one evidence line (a `path:line` or a source link). No input boxes, no copy-back. Questions print open.
- The `.unclear` part, its script, its CSS and `data-no-unclear` are gone.
- `house-style.html` shows the new questions part and a scene that builds its picture step by step with `data-show`.
